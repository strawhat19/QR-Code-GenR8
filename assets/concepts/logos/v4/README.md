# QR Code GenR8 logo concepts — v4

This round starts from `v2/02-link-matrix.svg` and explores red, blue, purple, and white with clearer GenR8 references.

- `01-linked-eight.svg` turns the two connected loops into a figure eight.
- `02-relay-r.svg` embeds an R in the linking action and retains QR-like pixel accents.
- `03-link-matrix-eight.svg` stays closest to the earlier link matrix, recolors its two links, and places an eight at the junction.

Palette: red `#FF3B5C`, blue `#3D70FF`, purple `#5E3BBE`, and white `#FFFFFF`. The marks are editable SVG explorations, not scannable QR codes.
